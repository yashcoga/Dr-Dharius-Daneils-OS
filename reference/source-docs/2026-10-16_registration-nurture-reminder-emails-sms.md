## Registration Follow-Up: Where Are You Upside Down?

**Suggested send:** Approximately 2 hours after the confirmation email.  
**Channel:** Email  
**Subject:** What are your relationships costing you?  
**Preview:** Before we meet, I want to ask you something.

Hi {{contact.first\_name}},

Now that you're registered, I want to talk about why we're doing this masterclass.

You can love the people in your life and still be exhausted by the way those relationships are working.

You can be surrounded by people and still feel like there is no one you can lean on.

You can be the person everyone calls and quietly wonder, “Who do I call?”

That's part of what I mean by being **upside down in your relationships**.

You're making the deposits. They're making the withdrawals. And after a while, you're running out of yourself.

Sometimes it looks like a family member whose emergencies keep becoming your responsibilities. Sometimes it's a coworker who can depend on your effort, but you can't depend on theirs. Sometimes it's a romantic relationship where you keep giving more, hoping that eventually you'll feel supported.

And here's what makes this difficult: **the relationship may matter deeply to you.**

So you keep showing up. You keep making room. You keep telling yourself, “Maybe if I do a little more, something will change.”

But more investment doesn't automatically create a different relationship.

Sometimes you're expecting something from a person that their pattern has never shown you they can give. Sometimes the person belongs in your life, but the way you're managing the relationship is costing you more than you can sustain.

That cost isn't always money. It's your time. Your attention. The energy you no longer have for other responsibilities. The frustration you carry into relationships that didn't cause it.

This isn't about making every relationship an equal exchange. A child needs care. Someone you're helping may not be able to give back what you give.

It's about understanding **what you're giving, why you're giving it, and what you can realistically expect**.

In **{{contact.ri\_masterclass\_name}}**, we'll look at where you're giving more than you can keep giving, what people's actions have shown you can expect from them, and what may need to change in how you handle those relationships.

That might mean no longer saying yes to every request. It might mean having a conversation you've been avoiding. Or it might mean no longer asking someone for support they've repeatedly shown they cannot give.

Before we meet, I want to ask you one question:

**Where do you feel most upside down in your relationships right now, and what is that pattern costing you?**

**Hit reply and tell me in a sentence or two.** It could be family, friendship, work, or a romantic relationship. No names or private details needed. Just tell me what you've been carrying.
