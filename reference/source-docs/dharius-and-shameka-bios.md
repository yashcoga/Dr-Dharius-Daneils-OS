# Bios: Dr. Dharius Daniels and Shameka Daniels

Source: bio PDF attached to the onboarding form (Sep 15, 2026). Extracted text, unedited.

Dharius’ Bio 
One of the most prolific communicators of our day, Dr. Dharius Daniels is on a mission to help as 
many people as possible become their best selves while doing their best work. Speaker, coach, and 
certified emotional intelligence specialist, Dharius specializes in helping others self optimize 
without self destructing. He acquired his passion to add value to the lives of others at an early age 
growing up in a small town, Kilmichael, MS. Although the population was less than 700 residents, 
he was taught by his father that your enlightenment, not your environment, determines your 
outcomes. He was inspired to unleash his untapped potential while gaining his undergraduate 
degree from Millsaps College and post graduate degrees from Princeton and Fuller. 
 
With over 20 years of leadership experience, Dharius understands that right information can cause 
a comprehensive revolution. This passion for transformation inspired him to continue his writing 
career by releasing his most recent published book Relational Intelligence: The People Skills You 
Need For The Life Of Purpose You Want. Dr. Dharius also emerged in the mentoring and coaching 
arena with the release of Daniels Den Mentoring and Real Life Seminary Coaching Programs, 
which serves thousands of members. 
 
In addition to his contributions in the field of speaking and coaching, Dharius continues to serve 
as Lead Pastor of the multi-site, and multi-regional, Change Church. He has also served as an 
adjunct professor at Princeton. He enjoys downtime with activities such as playing a round of golf 
or weight training. Dharius is married to his college sweetheart, Shameka, and they are the proud 
parents of two sons, Seth and Gabriel. 
 
 

Shameka’s Bio 
 
Shameka Daniels is a marriage and personal development coach committed to helping women 
live whole, anchored, and fulfilled lives. She works with women and couples who are ready to 
grow emotionally, spiritually, and relationally—without losing themselves in the process. 
Through coaching intensives, workshops, digital resources, and transformational experiences, 
Shameka helps women strengthen their inner life, deepen their marriages, and build disciplined, 
peaceful lives that reflect both faith and emotional maturity. Her approach blends biblical truth 
with practical strategy—because inspiration without application does not produce change. 
With a Master’s degree in Health & Human Counseling: Marriage and Family, Shameka brings 
both clinical insight and spiritual wisdom to her work. She is a certified Grief Recovery 
Specialist and a certified John C. Maxwell Coach, Teacher, Trainer, and Speaker. Her work 
focuses on emotional health, communication in marriage, boundaries, personal responsibility, 
and living intentionally. 
In addition to her coaching practice, Shameka serves in executive leadership at Change Church, 
where she helps lead organizational strategy and development. Her leadership experience in 
ministry, business operations, and family life uniquely equips her to speak into both private 
growth and public impact. 
A proud member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated, Shameka values sisterhood, 
service, and legacy. She is a devoted wife to her husband, Dharius, and a proud mother to their 
two sons. She enjoys travel, fitness, reading, and creating spaces where women can grow strong, 
soft, disciplined, and free.
