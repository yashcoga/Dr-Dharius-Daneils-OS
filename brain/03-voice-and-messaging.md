# Voice, Messaging and Compliance Rules

Last updated: 2026-10-16. These rules are drawn from the brief, the drafted copy (funnel and emails) and the Couples OS pages.

## PD's voice (as written in the approved-direction drafts)

- **Calm, pastoral-but-practical, first person** in emails ("I'll help you begin answering three questions"). Pages refer to him in the third person ("Dr. Daniels teaches…").
- **Plain words, short sentences, concrete scenes**, not abstractions: "the coworker whose responsibilities keep becoming yours," "the partner says they'll make more effort."
- **Balanced and non-blaming.** He always includes the reader's own part: "including your own choices and expectations," "This isn't a class about proving everyone else is the problem," "You don't have to decide that someone is bad to recognize that your expectations or investment need an adjustment."
- **Hope without naivety:** "You can hope for change without building every decision around it." / "People can grow. But there is a difference between leaving room for growth and making today's decisions as if that growth has already happened."
- **Reflection prompts** are a signature device: "Where do you feel most upside down…?" "What has this person consistently shown they can carry, and what have I been asking them to carry?" "That question is a starting point, not a verdict."
- **No hype, no exclamation-heavy selling, no fake scarcity.** The Couples OS upsells are slightly punchier ("That's not a commitment problem. It's a follow-through problem.") but stay respectful.

## Signature phrases (reuse; they are PD's language)

- "upside down in your relationships"
- "You're making all the deposits, and they're making all the withdrawals."
- overinvested · overcommitted · overextended
- placement and management · evaluate, place, manage
- "the five seats": friends, associates, assignments, advisors, family
- "Being needed isn't the same as being supported."
- "You are important to me, but you're not the only important thing to me."
- "what you expect and what you invest"
- "demonstrated behavior, not what you hope they will become"
- "You can be there for people without being everything to everyone."
- "Give without going empty."
- "We cannot improve what we don't evaluate." (a book quote)
- "running out of you" · "Who do I call?"

## Message angles (use across ads, pages and emails)

| Angle | Core tension | Used in |

| Upside down | Deposits vs. withdrawals; running out of you | Version C, emails |
| Needed ≠ supported | Everyone calls you; who do you call? | Emails, A problem section |

Contexts to rotate through: **work** (covering for coworkers), **family** (rescuing an adult child, a parent), **friendship** (always the one checking in) and **romance** (making all the effort).

## Hard compliance rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Education, not therapy or counseling.** Every page footer must say so: "This masterclass is educational, not therapy or individualized counseling."
2. **Never promise to fix relationships** or guarantee how other people will respond. Use "You cannot guarantee how someone else responds. You can get clearer about the decisions that belong to you."
3. **Never invent the Family seat's criteria.** Name it only.
4. **No main-offer specifics** (name, price, inclusions, schedule or refund) until the offer sheet is final. Use `[PROGRAM NAME]`, `[PRICE]` and similar placeholders.
5. **Don't promise** private PD access, answers to individual questions, AI tools, certification or unconfirmed bonuses.
6. **No results guarantee** (PD rejected one). Refund language exists for the Prep Kit only: 30 days.
7. **Privacy reassurance:** attendees never need to share names or private details ("Keep your notes private").
8. **Crisis language** appears on the Prep Kit page and is recommended for any page with deeper content: "If you are experiencing abuse, a mental health crisis, or thoughts of self-harm, contact a licensed professional or call or text 988. In immediate danger, call 911. National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233."
9. **SMS consent** is a separate, optional, unticked checkbox with STOP/HELP language and terms and privacy links. Consent is not required to register.
10. **Not a church program.** Avoid church or ministry framing on RI pages unless PD approves it.
11. **Individuals, not couples.** Don't reuse Couples OS language like "both partners," "two seats" or "us."

## Copy tensions spotted in the drafts (raise before finalizing)

- The Prep Kit headline "Walk Into the Masterclass Already Knowing **Who's Draining You**" and the thank-you step "the three people **who take the most out of you**" lean more blaming than PD's balanced voice. The emails frame the same step as "three people or relationships **you want greater clarity about**." Recommend aligning these.
- The Prep Kit promises "about **20 minutes**" of prep, but the teaching length is still `[LENGTH]`. If the teaching runs longer, the 20-minute claim breaks.
- "The night that can change how you handle all of it" is fine as aspiration, but keep it from drifting into an outcome promise.
