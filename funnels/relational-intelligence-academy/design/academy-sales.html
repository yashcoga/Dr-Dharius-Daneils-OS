Evaluate · Place · Manage

Here's Exactly What's Inside The Relational Intelligence Academy

The Academy follows the three steps you learned at the masterclass: Evaluate, Place and Manage. Each week has a prerecorded training, a live coaching call and a practical assignment, so by Week 6 you have a written plan for your own relationships.

Week 1 · Evaluate

The Relational Audit

  • Where am I upside down?
  • Why am I upside down?
  • What is it costing me?
  • What needs to change?
What you walk away with

The Relational Equity Audit, a clear diagnosis of the three relationships costing you the most.

Week 2 · Place

The Principles of Relational Placement

  • The five types of relationships: family, friends, associates, assignments and advisors
  • The five placements: core, close, casual, cautious and closed
  • The five C's that decide where someone belongs: character, capacity, consistency, compatibility and commitment
  • Why people end up in the wrong place
What you walk away with

Your Relational Placement Map, with every important person classified and placed.

Week 3 · Place

The Practice of Relational Repositioning

  • Who needs to be repositioned
  • The five kinds of repositioning: promotion, preservation, reduction, restriction and removal
  • What makes repositioning hard, and how to work through it
  • The steps to reposition someone well
What you walk away with

Your Relational Repositioning Plan for the one or two relationships that need it most.

Week 4 · Manage

Managing Difficult Conversations

  • Which conversations need to happen
  • How to prepare
  • How to have them clearly and honestly
  • How to handle resistance, guilt, blame shifting and requests to go back
What you walk away with

Your Difficult Conversation Blueprint for the conversation you've been avoiding.

Week 5 · Manage

Managing Burdens and Boundaries

  • The difference between supporting someone and carrying them
  • Releasing burdens that aren't yours
  • What boundaries are and what they aren't
  • Setting boundaries and keeping them when they're tested
What you walk away with

Your Burdens and Boundaries Plan: what you'll stop carrying, the boundaries you'll set and how you'll keep them.

Week 6 · Manage

Managing Separation and Elimination

  • Distance versus disconnection
  • When separation is appropriate
  • Why forgiveness doesn't automatically restore trust or access
  • Handling grief, guilt and shared relationships afterward
What you walk away with

Your Relational Management Plan, a responsible strategy for the conversations, boundaries and decisions ahead.

What if I can't make a live call?

Every call is recorded, so you can watch the replay anytime.

How much time does it take each week?

1 to 2 hours per week.

I've read Relational Intelligence. Is this the same?

The book covers four types of relationships and how to discern them. The Academy adds family, the five placements and the whole Manage step: the conversation, the boundary and the hard decision.

Is this only for romantic relationships?

No. It covers family, friends, coworkers, ministry and romantic relationships.

Is this therapy or counseling?

No. It's coaching and education built on my teaching, and it doesn't replace therapy, counseling or medical care.
